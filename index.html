Financial Notes

How to interpret this prototype

“Assessed” represents the illustrative additional liability recorded against a case. “Recovered” represents the illustrative amount collected. “Outstanding” is calculated as assessed minus recovered and does not account for appeals, adjustments, write-offs, penalties, interest or payment plans.
